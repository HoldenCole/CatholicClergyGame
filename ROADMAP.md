# Roadmap

Things the owner has chosen to build later. Each entry says what it is for, so the design does not have to be rediscovered.

## Health, not just strain

Strain wears the man but nothing ever breaks. Age and strain together should produce health:

- A `health` value on the character, drifting down with age past 55 and faster while strain stays above the worn line, recovering with rest and the running club.
- Complications as authored events, never mechanics to optimize: a collapse in Holy Week that costs the Triduum; a hospital stay of some weeks that hands the parish to the vicar and stalls a project; a diagnosis at sixty that shapes the retirement letter; a heart that stops early for a man who never rested, so that death can come before seventy-five to the careless.
- The bishop reads health: a sick pastor is moved to a smaller parish "for his own good," which is content, not failure.
- The settings dial for wear should also govern how fast health follows strain, so a player who wants a game about the work can turn it off.

## Save slots and a shelf of finished lives

- Several named save slots beside the autosave, with the seed and the week shown; export and import already exist.
- A shelf of finished careers: the summary the ending screen writes, the diocese, the years, the posts held, kept in the browser and exportable, so replaying different men is part of the game.
- A "play this man again" from the shelf: same seed and creation answers, a fresh run.

## Custom dioceses

The five presets are plain data files in the schema a custom diocese would use (CLAUDE.md rule 5), so an in-game editor is mostly UI: name, see, region, size, wealth, media exposure, Rome connection, Latino share, growth, disposition and shortage biases, institutions, a flavor pool, and a dozen real parishes with `real` seeds. Save the result as a preset in the browser and offer it on the diocese cards beside the five. Kept on the roadmap; real churches for the five went in first.

## A word with the people

"Have a word" reaches the pastor, the staff, the bishop, group leaders, and classmates. The named parishioners the parish now remembers (bonds) should be next: three bands of exchanges for `parishioner`, with the bond phrase in the prose, so the widow whose husband you buried can be sat with.

## Run to the next thing that asks

One button that advances until a letter, a scene, or a beat needs the man, and hotkeys for the week and for each sheet. The week button is pressed hundreds of times in a life.

## A spiritual director for the priest himself

Choose one (a classmate, the old pastor, a monk from the house the parish leans on) and keep a monthly hour with him. It steadies piety and the doubt line. Authored only, never through the LLM, per §11.

## The sacramental register

A sheet of the year's baptisms, weddings, and funerals by name, drawn from the bonds, with anniversaries that come back as small scenes a decade on.

## Money of your own

Stipend, savings, a car that dies, the family asking for help, and retirement savings that decide whether the ending is a rectory room or a condo by the water.

## The school as a place

A principal, a board, a graduation each June, and the closure as a fought arc rather than a project bar.

## Retirement as a coda

Senior priest status, weekend supply, the priests' home, the memoir, the classmates' funerals. A short last act before the shelf page.

## The diocese's scandal as an authored arc

The latent scandal breaks, the chancery handles it in its style, the parish reacts, the bishop may fall. Authored only, per §11, and never with the player as the one accused.

## The Roman end

Cardinal and conclave, on the post-V1 list: a nunciature posting, the red hat, and a conclave played as a sequence of scenes.

## Content packs

Drop a JSON file of events or offers into the running game, validated in the browser with the same checks the tests run, so writing scenes never needs a build.
